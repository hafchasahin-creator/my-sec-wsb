CORONAVIRUS

What it is · how it spreads · how it stops

Spike proteins

Club-shaped keys ringing the shell like a crown — corona is Latin for crown.

Lipid envelope

A fatty bubble holding it together. It is not alive, and soap tears it apart.

RNA genome

About 30,000 letters of code. That is the whole virus: instructions in a shell.
