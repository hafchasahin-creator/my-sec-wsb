HOW TO PLAY

THE GAME

  • Goal: clear 8 rooms alive.
  • One rule: pick LIVE, not DIE.
  • 120 seconds per room. The timer runs out, you die.
  • Search the room for clues before choosing. The obvious answer is often the trap.
  • Walk to a door and hold interact for 1.5s to commit. Tapping does nothing.
  • One hit is death. You have 3 lives, then it all starts again at Room 1.
