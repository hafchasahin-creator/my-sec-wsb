Every model and texture shipped in `assets/` is derived from an openly licensed
source. Files were re-encoded (WebP textures, meshopt-compressed geometry) but
not otherwise altered in substance. Attribution required by the licences below
is also shown in the experience itself, under **Credits** in the HUD menu.
