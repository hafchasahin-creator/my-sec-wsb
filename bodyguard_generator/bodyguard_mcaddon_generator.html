One-tap .mcaddon generator for Minecraft Bedrock mobile
Built for 1.21.0.26 beta (works on 1.20.60+) · no Experiments needed

Tap the button — the finished addon downloads instantly.

📦 Install (once downloaded)

  1. Open the downloaded Bodyguard_Companion.mcaddon — from your notification bar or Files/Downloads — and choose Minecraft to open it.
  2. Minecraft launches and imports both packs (behavior + resource) automatically.
  3. Create or edit a world → Behavior Packs → Available → Bodyguard Companion → Activate. The resource pack activates by itself (it is a dependency).

If your browser saves it as .zip, rename the file to end in .mcaddon in the Files app, then open it.

🤝 Spawn & bond your bodyguard

  1. Find the Bodyguard Spawn Egg in Creative (search “Bodyguard”), or with cheats: /give @s bgc:bodyguard_spawn_egg  /  /summon bgc:bodyguard
  2. Tap the ground with the egg to spawn your guard.
  3. Bond it (one tap): hold any food, an emerald, iron ingot, gold ingot, diamond, stick or bone — the guard walks over to you — then tap it (a Tame button appears). Hearts = it’s yours forever.

Bedrock’s stable (non-experimental) engine can’t assign an owner at spawn without scripts, so bonding is a single tap — after that everything below is 100% automatic.

🗡️ What your bodyguard does

  • Follows you everywhere and sprints to keep up
  • Teleports back to you if it falls behind, gets stuck, or you travel far
  • Instantly attacks anything that damages you — and anything you attack
  • Hunts nearby zombies, skeletons, creepers, spiders, pillagers & all hostile mobs on sight
  • Fights until the threat is dead, then returns to your side
  • Never attacks you, other players, or tamed/friendly mobs
  • 300 ❤ health, heavy knockback resistance, no fall damage, 12 ⚔ sword damage
  • On-screen health bar (boss-bar style) once bonded
  • Custom knight model with sword + walk / idle / attack / hurt animations
  • Guarding always beats wandering — it only strolls when you’re safe
  • Feed it bread / cooked meat / golden apples to heal it
